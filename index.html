The feeling of being here

A space between places.

Aruna began with a simple thought: the best parts of a day often happen when we give them room. A good cup in both hands. A conversation that takes its time. A table you can stay at awhile.

So we made a neighborhood space for all of it. Our coffee is carefully sourced and thoughtfully made; our welcome is for everyone. Come as you are, stay as long as you like.
